Pest control Abu Dhabi villa owners trust has to cover both the house and the garden, because the two feed each other.

Ants in villas and gardens

Sugar ants trailing across kitchen counters usually nest outside — in garden soil, under pavers or in wall voids. Spraying the visible trail kills foragers but not the colony. Proper ant control uses bait that workers carry back to the queen, plus sealing the entry points they use. Pharaoh ants, common in the UAE, need the same baiting approach — repellent sprays just split the colony and make it worse.

Mosquitoes and outdoor comfort

Standing water in saucers, fountains and irrigation boxes breeds mosquitoes within days in summer. Treatment combines larvicide in water features, fogging of dense planting, and — most importantly — removing breeding sites. A garden you can't sit in during the evening usually has a water problem, not just a mosquito problem. https://bitepestcontrol.com

Termites in villa woodwork

Subterranean termites come up from the soil into wooden door frames, skirting and pergolas. Early signs are mud tubes along walls and hollow-sounding wood. Treatment means soil treatment around the foundation and direct treatment of infested timber — surface spraying alone won't reach the colony underground.

Cockroaches, rodents and general protection

Villa kitchens, maid rooms and outdoor kitchens attract German and American cockroaches; boundary walls and garden sheds shelter rodents. A quarterly villa plan — inspection, targeted treatment, entry-point sealing — keeps the whole property protected through every season.
